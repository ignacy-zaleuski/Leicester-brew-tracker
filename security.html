Night Shift: café check-in pilot

Before the Brew Tracker refreshed its Leicester café listings, the team trialled an after-hours check-in for partner shops. The nightly sync collected a manager's voice memo and a short counter recording, then saved both with the directory's backup note. The pilot was shelved, but its last check-in was never cleared from the archive.

Note from the product archive

“Those two recordings belong to one check-in. I remember planning a Vigenère routine for the paired notes, but the handover never made it into the directory docs. There's one last field in the backup after them.”
